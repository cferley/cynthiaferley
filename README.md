## About Me

I'm a data analyst with a background that spans military service, corporate operations, and health informatics. I spent a decade in the U.S. Army as a Healthcare Specialist — managing patient data, deploying to combat hospitals, and serving as the sole medic for a convo and recon team. After the Army, I joined IGT, where I advanced from Computer Operator to Data Operations Analyst II, supporting SOC I/II compliance, developing training programs, and conducting log audits on Linux servers.

I turn messy data into clear decisions. I completed my B.S. in Health Information and Informatics Management at Texas State University and am open to data analyst roles across industries.
